# AuditAI - EOD Report (Feb 18, 2026)

### 🚀 Key Feature Additions
*   **🏗️ Schema Architect:**
    *   **Purpose:** Builds error-free JSON-LD Schema markup for Rich Snippets (Stars, FAQ, Products).
    *   **Value:** Automates a highly technical task that usually requires manual coding; improves Search Engine visibility instantly.
*   **⚔️ Content Conflict AI:**
    *   **Purpose:** Identifies internal keyword cannibalization where two of your pages fight for the same rank.
    *   **Value:** Provides "Merge" or "Redirect" strategy based on Search Intent, preventing you from losing rankings to your own content.
*   **📍 Local Command Center:**
    *   **Purpose:** Audits Google My Business (GMB) and identifies "Neighborhood-Level" keywords.
    *   **Value:** Includes an **AI Review Responder** to draft professional replies, saving hours of reputation management.
*   **💀 Toxicity Scanner:**
    *   **Purpose:** Scans backlink profiles for "Spam" signals and PBN patterns.
    *   **Value:** Explains *why* a link is toxic and generates a Disavow file to protect your site from Google penalties.
